## Method note

This bundle validates 2026 team totals for all 15 teams and outputs player-year contract/status rows. The Phoenix Mercury raw HTML was available as a stable file, so its buyout logic is preserved exactly from the HTML.

For the remaining teams, the duplicate upload path was not stable enough to safely re-parse every submitted outerHTML block in the local filesystem. I retained the prior all-team cleaned extraction, then solved/inferred contract indicators against Her Hoop Stats source totals. The provenance fields `source_quality` and `contract_icon_source` identify whether a row is raw-HTML exact or solved/inferred.

## Important caveat

Rows marked `source_quality = exact_raw_outerhtml` preserve icons directly from HTML classes/tooltips. Rows marked `prior_csv_inferred` are validated against team totals, but icon classification is solved/inferred rather than directly preserved from local HTML. This keeps the dataset analytically useful without pretending every icon was recovered perfectly.
